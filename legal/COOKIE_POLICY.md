# Cookie Policy for Weave

Last updated: 2026-05-08

This Cookie Policy explains how Weave uses cookies and similar technologies.

## 1) What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser/device to help websites function, remember settings, and understand usage.

## 2) Cookies We Use

Based on current implementation, Weave uses first-party cookies for gameplay state and user experience:

- `weave_daily_lock_v1`  
  Stores whether your daily game is locked after completion for a specific day/category scope.

- `weave_daily_result_v1`  
  Stores your current daily result state (placements, misses, elapsed time) so gameplay can persist.

- `weave_signin_nudge_dismissed_v1`  
  Stores whether you dismissed the sign-in prompt for the current daily scope.

- `weave_daily_events_v1`  
  Stores selected daily event IDs by scope so your puzzle remains consistent across refreshes.

These cookies are set with `path=/` and `samesite=lax`.

## 3) Analytics Technologies

We use Vercel Analytics for product metrics and event tracking. Depending on implementation details and browser behavior, analytics may use cookies or similar measurement mechanisms.

## 4) Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting or blocking them. Blocking some cookies may affect game functionality (for example, saved progress and daily lock behavior).

## 5) Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as the product evolves. We will update the "Last updated" date when material changes are made.
