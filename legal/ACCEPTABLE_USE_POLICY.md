# Acceptable Use Policy for Weave

Last updated: 2026-05-08

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") is part of the Weave Terms of Service and describes prohibited uses of the service.

## 1) Prohibited Conduct

You may not use Weave to:

- Violate any law or regulation.
- Harass, threaten, abuse, or impersonate others.
- Upload illegal, defamatory, obscene, hateful, or otherwise harmful content.
- Upload content that infringes intellectual property or privacy rights.
- Distribute malware, malicious code, or harmful files.
- Interfere with service security, integrity, or availability.
- Attempt unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or data.
- Use bots, scraping, or automation in a way that harms service performance or bypasses controls.
- Engage in fraud, deception, or other abusive behavior.

## 2) Avatar and Profile Content

If you upload avatars, you must ensure content:

- Is lawful and non-infringing.
- Does not include malware or exploit payloads.
- Is appropriate for a general audience.

We may remove content that violates this policy.

## 3) Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take action including:

- Warning.
- Content removal.
- Temporary restriction.
- Account suspension or termination.
- Reporting to relevant authorities when required.

## 4) Reporting Abuse

To report abuse or policy violations:

- Email: weavesupport@gmail.com
- Include: account details, screenshots/links, and a brief description of the issue.

## 5) Changes

We may update this AUP from time to time. Continued use of Weave after updates means you accept the revised policy.
